### 🚀 Architecture Expansion V2.0
<img width="4765" height="1778" alt="PCME_RAM_V2_Simulation" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c5604d6a-031d-4b64-95fd-3a508bea734d" />

[PCME-RAM_V2.0_Phased-Array_Acoustic_Beamforming_&_Pre-Biasing](PCME-RAM_V2.0_Phased-Array_Acoustic_Beamforming_&_Pre-Biasing.pdf)

**Update Overview:** 
The V2.0 expansion introduces a fundamental paradigm shift by applying radar-based **Phased-Array Acoustic Beamforming** and **Magneto-Elastic Pre-Biasing** to the nanoscale architecture. 
* **Bandwidth Explosion:** By segmenting the transducer layer into an active array, acoustic waves are focused into highly directional beams. This completely eliminates signal crosstalk via destructive interference, allowing for maximum cell density and scaling the theoretical bandwidth up to **13.68 TB/s**.
* **Sub-Nanosecond Latency:** Applying a static DC voltage creates a permanent mechanical pre-tension in the magnetic layer. This pre-biasing navigates the memory state to the critical tipping point, requiring only a minimal acoustic impulse to switch, thus reducing theoretical latency to **< 1.2 ns**.

Conclusion

The PCME-RAM concept defines a highly innovative, hybrid memory architecture. It combines established physical principles (magnetoelectrics, piezoelectrics, elastodynamics) into a novel information technology construct. Through the integration of acoustic impedance and attenuation models, LLG dynamics, fundamental thermal FEM equations, realistic yield models, and the numerical modeling framework, this document operates as a comprehensive and validatable blueprint for the PCME research program within the Open Origin Architecture.
