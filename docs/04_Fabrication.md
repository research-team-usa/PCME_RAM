# Fabrication Protocol (TRL 3 / TRL 4)

1.  **Transducer:** Reactive magnetron sputtering of AlN at $350^\circ C$.
2.  **Bottom-Electrode:** Deposition of SrRuO3 (Pulsed Laser Deposition).
3.  **Heterostructure:** Combined deposition of BaTiO3 and CoFe2O4 under controlled oxygen partial pressure.
4.  **Patterning:** DUV lithography and ion beam etching (IBE).
5.  **Passivation & Microchannels:** ALD passivation and wet-chemical KOH etching on the wafer backside.
6.  **Coupling:** Spin-coating of the h-BN epoxy matrix followed by UV curing.
