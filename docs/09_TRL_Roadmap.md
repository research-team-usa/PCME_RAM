# TRL Roadmap – PCME-RAM Development Path

PCME-RAM follows a staged Technology Readiness Level (TRL) roadmap from fundamental cell validation  
to multi-layer engineering samples with integrated microfluidics and acoustic multiplexing.

---

## TRL 3 – Single-Cell Verification

- **Objective:** Verify magnetoelectric memory cell behavior at the single-cell level.
- **Methods:**
  - MOKE (Magneto-Optical Kerr Effect) for magnetic statics and switching.
  - PFM (Piezoresponse Force Microscopy) for strain and piezoelectric coupling.
- **Focus:**
  - Confirm non-volatile switching.
  - Validate strain-mediated magnetization control.
  - Establish baseline switching times and energy.

---

## TRL 4 – 8×8 Array Demonstrator

- **Objective:** Scale from single cell to a small array (8×8) to study interactions.
- **Methods:**
  - Acoustic impedance measurements across multiple cells.
  - Crosstalk characterization between adjacent acoustic channels.
- **Focus:**
  - Channel isolation and signal integrity.
  - Practical limits of acoustic multiplexing at small scale.
  - Validation of impedance matching and phononic mitigation concepts.

---

## TRL 5 – 4-Layer Stack with Microfluidics

- **Objective:** Introduce vertical stacking and integrated microfluidic cooling.
- **Methods:**
  - Transient thermal FEM simulations vs. measured thermal behavior.
  - Capillary-driven microchannel cooling performance.
- **Focus:**
  - Heat flux density and cooling capacity.
  - Stability of microfluidic operation (dry-out, flow distribution).
  - Mechanical and thermal integrity of stacked layers.

---

## TRL 6 – 16-Layer Engineering Sample

- **Objective:** Realize a full 16-layer PCME-RAM stack approaching architectural targets.
- **Methods:**
  - Empirical yield measurements across full wafer runs.
  - Bandwidth and latency characterization vs. theoretical FDM capacity model.
- **Focus:**
  - Practical yield vs. economic model assumptions.
  - End-to-end signal path (acoustic + magnetoelectric + microfluidics).
  - Identification of scaling bottlenecks and industrialization risks.

---

This roadmap defines the **experimental path** from concept to engineering demonstrator,  
while explicitly acknowledging that PCME-RAM remains a **research architecture** and not yet  
a validated commercial memory technology.
