# Economic & Yield Models

Cost projections are based on theoretical yield models assuming the elimination of extreme Cu-Cu hybrid bonding processes:

**Poisson Model:** $Y = e^{-DA}$  
**Murphy Model:** $Y = \left(\frac{1 - e^{-DA}}{DA}\right)^2$
