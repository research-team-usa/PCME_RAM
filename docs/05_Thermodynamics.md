# Thermodynamics and Microfluidics (FEM Basis)

The capillary pressure generated within the microchannel (Young-Laplace) is given by:

$$\Delta P_{cap}=\frac{2\gamma\cos(\theta_c)}{r_{eff}}\approx10.71\,kPa$$

For future thermal Finite Element Method (FEM) analyses, the transient heat conduction equation is applied:

$$\rho c_p \frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = \nabla(k\nabla T) + Q$$

*Simulation Expectation:* Target cooling capacity: $>520\,W/cm^2$ (subject to future experimental validation). Validation of dry-out parameters is pending.
